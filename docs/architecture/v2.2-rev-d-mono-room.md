# AudioPicture V2.2 Rev.D — Mono two-way, room software

Status: **ARCHITECTURE_AUTHORITY / NOT_AN_ACOUSTIC_MEASUREMENT**

This revision replaces Rev.C. One picture is one excellent mono loudspeaker. Stereo, width, and coverage come from two or more pictures in the same Home Assistant room.

Footprint stays **320 × 400 mm**. Body thickness stays **84 mm maximum**. The motors did not get shorter.

## 1. One picture

The box is a two-way with a passive radiator, not a DML and not a stereo pair in one frame.

| Role | Part | Qty | Data used |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Woofer | Dayton Audio ND91-4 | 1 | 4 ohm, Fs 74 Hz, Qts 0.41, Vas 1.4 L, Sd 30.4 cm², Xmax 4.6 mm, 85.6 dB @ 2.83 V/1 m, 30 W RMS, mounting depth 56.8 mm |
| Tweeter | Dayton Audio ND20FB-4 | 1 | 19 mm textile dome, 4 ohm, 90 dB @ 2.83 V/1 m, 15 W RMS, Fs about 2.1 kHz, overall depth 19 mm |
| Passive radiator | Dayton Audio ND90-PR | 2 | Sd 31.2 cm² each, Xmax 9 mm, Vas 1.5 L, overall depth 60.7 mm |

Two ND90-PR displace about 56 cm³. One ND91 displaces about 12 cm³. The passives stay above twice the woofer volume.

The ND91 is crossed to the dome. It is not used to 17 kHz.

Layout on the baffle, one sealed volume:

- tweeter on the vertical centreline, slightly above mid-height;
- woofer on the same centreline, centre-to-centre **≤ 70 mm**;
- one passive above, one below, both offset so their distances to the edges are not equal multiples;
- electronics and microphones in a sealed pocket that does not share air with the drivers.

Net driver air: **2.5–4.0 L**. That is the whole 320 × 400 × 84 mm body minus walls, braces, and the electronics pocket. Rev.C split the same shell into two 1.0–1.3 L rooms; that split is withdrawn so this woofer has the volume it needs.

First box model, done: [`mechanical/acoustic-box-model-rev-d.md`](../../mechanical/acoustic-box-model-rev-d.md). It gives **4.12 L** net air and one supplied **7.3 g** disk on each ND90-PR. After DSP the woofer branch is **F3 50.6 Hz** and **55–500 Hz ±0.8 dB**, with about **88 dB** flat maximum at 1 m. The earlier "F3 55–60 Hz before DSP" target is replaced by a DSP-aware tuning rule. Bench measurement is still required. The wall is the baffle. No port, no rear chimney.

Thickness is unchanged from Rev.C: cloth 1.5 mm, 12.2 mm of front air for +9 mm passive excursion, 61 mm of ND90-PR behind the baffle face, 3 mm magnet clearance, 5 mm rear wall. Hard maximum **84 mm**. The limiter holds the woofer to 4.6 mm and each passive to 9 mm.

## 2. Amp and crossover

TAS5825M stays, 48 kHz, +24 V. Both BTL channels are used as an **active** two-way:

- channel A: ND91-4;
- channel B: ND20FB-4, padded and high-passed in DSP.

Acoustic crossover **3 kHz**, at least fourth-order Linkwitz–Riley in the TAS DSP. No passive crossover in the signal path. The Rev.A 10 µH / 0.68 µF network is retuned per 4 ohm load; do not copy the 8 ohm exciter values.

DSP jobs on this unit, before any room group:

- wall-baffle response, not a free-standing baffle-step curve;
- cut the ND91 breakup above the crossover;
- bass boost only inside woofer and passive excursion;
- dynamic bass limiter: the +6 dB 67 Hz boost backs off as level rises (open);
- factory curve stored on the device.

Reference implementation: `firmware/components/room_audio` (`ap_dsp`). It is host-tested float C at 48 kHz: subsonic BW4 45 Hz, model EQ, LR4 3 kHz, role routing, delay ≤ 50 ms, fail-closed configuration. It still has to be ported to the TAS5825M coefficient RAM.

PoE does not get the external 24 V level. The excursion limiter is the same on both supplies.

## 3. Design objectives, one picture

Bars for the box model and the first measured sample. They are not results.

- 55 Hz–20 kHz within ±3 dB at 1 m, on the centre axis, at 80 dB, after the factory curve.
- 90 dB short-term at 1 m from 100 Hz up, inside the 30 W woofer rating.
- One electrical program: L+R downmix, or the assigned stereo channel when paired.
- No cloth contact at the limited excursion.
- Below 50 Hz the objective stops. Two 3.5 inch passives in a few litres do not support a 45 Hz ±3 dB claim.

## 4. Several pictures in one room

Home Assistant is the only pairing UI. No user YAML. A picture that is alone always plays a mono downmix.

### 4.1 Stereo pair

Exactly two identical AudioPicture units, same firmware major version, same room:

- one is **LEFT**, one is **RIGHT**;
- each plays only its channel, time-aligned;
- the HA room shows one media player;
- recommended placement: 2.0–3.0 m apart, listening position 2.0–3.5 m in front, matching Sonos’s stereo-pair geometry.

A picture and a different brand cannot form this pair.

### 4.2 Room group

Three or more units in the same HA room, or two units that are not a stereo pair:

- one shared clock;
- one shared stream;
- per-unit delay and level so arrivals match at a chosen listening zone;
- optional: two of them remain the stereo pair, the others play a mono fill with extra delay so they do not smear the image.

Playing the same downmix on every unit without delay matching is not the product behaviour.

### 4.3 Clock and transport

- Sample clock follows a common time base (PTP on Ethernet when wired, otherwise a Wi-Fi time protocol with measured path delay). Target: **< 50 µs** relative error inside a stereo pair, **< 200 µs** inside a room group.
- Audio is sent as a room stream, not as N independent phone Bluetooth links.
- Start/stop/volume/source are atomic for the group.

Reference implementation:

- `ap_clock`: four-timestamp exchanges, 64-exchange window, lowest-delay eighth fitted for offset and drift, lock only below a residual bound. On a synthetic link with 50 ppm drift and 0.3–1.8 ms one-way jitter it holds **< 25 µs** at +250 ms.
- `ap_room`: validates the room plan with the same rules as above.
- `home-assistant/custom_components/audiopicture/room.py` computes delays and trims from listener distances: arrival alignment at 343 m/s, 1/r level match, fill +10 ms / −3 dB by default.

None of this is wired to I2S, the network, or HA entities yet.

### 4.4 Calibration

Use the four product microphones as a measurement path. Raw samples bypass AEC, AGC, noise suppression, and beamforming.

Sequence, run from HA, room quiet:

1. identity and polarity check of every unit in the room;
2. log sweep or MLS from each unit in turn;
3. per-unit magnitude/phase at the listening zone (phone or a designated AudioPicture mic);
4. write FIR/IIR and delay into each TAS5825M;
5. stereo-pair check: L-only, R-only, L+R, L−R;
6. store a named snapshot on the units and in HA.

DSP may flatten a repeatable curve. It may not add bass beyond the excursion model. A failed calibration leaves the factory curve in place.

Entities on one HA device still include media player, Assist, temperature, humidity, illuminance, presence, network, power, firmware, and calibration state. The room group adds: role (solo / left / right / fill), group id, delay, last calibration, clock lock.

## 5. Everything else

Voice, radar, light, temperature, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, PoE, and OTA stay. They sit in the sealed electronics pocket. They do not share driver air and they do not sit on a cone.

Cloth over the drivers: ShowTex Speaker Mesh Print, item 3838. No metallic ink.

Hanger on the rear wall, sized for **8 kg** and a **3×** static pull until a prototype is weighed. The cones carry none of the weight.

Playback, grouping, and calibration are **not** in the current diagnostic Home Assistant integration. They are product requirements from this revision.

## 6. Withdrawn

- Left and right in one picture, the 248 mm tweeter spacing, and the 16 mm acoustic spine.
- Two 1.0–1.3 L chambers.
- Passive crossover after Class-D.
- The DML sandwich and both Dayton exciters, already withdrawn in Rev.C.
- The 48 mm body.
