## 3. Panel and motors

Panel blank: **296 × 286 × 8 mm**, phenolic or aramid honeycomb, areal density 1.5–2.2 kg/m². The frame opening is 16 mm smaller on each side so the panel rests on a rebate. Origin for the coordinates below is the lower-left corner of the panel, X to the right, Y up.

| Ref | Part | Load | Power | Place (X, Y) mm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| EX_LF | Billionsound **RMD002514H26-BS** | 4 Ω | 5–10 W | 110, 78 |
| EX_WB | Tectonic **TEAX25C10-8/HS** | 8 Ω | 10 W | 186, 200 |

TEAX25 data used: 61.3 × 50.8 × 19 mm, 85 g, Fs 202 Hz, stated band 100 Hz–20 kHz. RMD data used: diameter 46.8 mm, height 15.5 ± 0.5 mm, 4 Ω. If the RMD part cannot be bought, the replacement must be ≤ 16 mm, 4 Ω, ≥ 8 W, and must publish an Fs below 150 Hz. An unpublished Fs, or an Fs above 200 Hz, is a reject.

Do not stack the motors. Do not wire them in parallel. Do not put either on the geometric centre.

Cloth: ShowTex Speaker Mesh 3838, no metallic ink, 340 × 420 mm before wrap.

## 4. Amplifier

The published Satellite1 firmware (`speaker.yaml` in FutureProofHomes/Satellite1-ESPHome) sends I2S from **GPIO9** to the onboard DAC proxy: TAS2780 at 0x3F for the kit speaker, PCM5122 at 0x4D for the jack. There is no documented I2S port for an external TAS5825M. That part is withdrawn.

The external path that the kit does publish is the **3.5 mm line-out**.

| Jack channel | After a 300 Hz line-level split | Motor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Left | low-pass, plus a 45 Hz high-pass | EX_LF, 4 Ω |
| Right | high-pass | EX_WB, 8 Ω |

The amplifier is a stereo analog Class-D board, line input, powered at 20 V, happy with 4 Ω on one channel and 8 Ω on the other. No bass boost until a 1 m sweep exists. Continuous limit 8 W per motor. The kit TAS2780 stays unconnected.

Frequencies are recorded in `firmware/components/room_audio/include/ap_model_curve_rev_g.h`.

## 5. Power

| Rail | Source | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HAT + Core | USB-C PD supply from the Dev Kit, into the HAT port marked for the ESP | The kit negotiates its own voltage |
| External amplifier | Separate **20 V, 3 A** supply | The kit supply stays on the HAT. Grounds meet at one point |
| Ground | One bond between the TAS ground and the HAT ground | No second bond through the USB shields |

A 45 W PD brick on the HAT does not, by itself, power the TAS. Sharing one brick is allowed only after the HAT schematic shows a 20 V tap that stays up while the kit enumerates.

## 6. Electronics bay

Bottom bay interior **312 × 98 mm**, deeper than the 10 mm boards. A 74 mm bay was checked and rejected: the HAT is 88 mm.

| Part | Size | Place |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HAT | 88 × 88 × 10 mm | Left side of the bay, buttons and mics toward the room |
| Core | 66 × 31 × 10 mm | Right of the HAT, antenna cable free of the panel bond |
| LD2450 | 15 × 44 mm | Behind the front window, antenna at the cloth, no metal over it |
| Analog amplifier | Inside the bay | 3.5 mm lead from the HAT jack. Twisted pairs up to the two exciters |
| USB-C | Bottom-edge slot, 14 × 8 mm | HAT supply only |

Microphone slots in the STL are a 20 mm pitch. Move them to the real HAT capsules before the second print. The first print is a fit check.

Mute is the HAT hardware mute. The 24-LED ring is not required for this product.

## 7. What “good” means for this build

Pass, on the first measured sample, at 1 m on axis:

- 80 Hz–15 kHz within **±5 dB** after the provisional DSP at 80 dB SPL.
- 70 Hz no more than **6 dB** below the level at 200 Hz.
- 90 dB for 10 s from 200 Hz to 2 kHz without a fault or a rattle.
- A voice command understood at 3 m while the panel plays 80 dB.
- LD2450 reports a person at 2 m through the window.

The goal, not the pass gate, remains 50–60 Hz and ±3 dB. This catalog pair does not guarantee that.

## 9. Order of work

1. Print the frame. Drop in HAT, Core, and both plugs. Nothing stands past 30 mm.
2. Play a tone into the line-out. Confirm the left channel reaches EX_LF and the right channel reaches EX_WB.
3. Bond EX_LF and EX_WB at the coordinates in §3. Sweep before the cloth.
4. Add cloth. Run the pass checks in §7.
5. Fit the LD2450 and tune its zones.
6. Only then change the panel material or the bass exciter. Do not add a third motor.
