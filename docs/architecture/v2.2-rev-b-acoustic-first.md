# AudioPicture V2.2 Rev.B — Acoustic-first architecture

Status: **SUPERSEDED_BY_REV_C**

Stereo two-way authority is `docs/architecture/v2.2-rev-c-stereo.md`. The single-exciter DML below is withdrawn.

This revision replaced Rev.A wherever the two disagreed. Rev.EN–Rev.FI remain historical evidence of the old four-exciter frame. They do not choose the radiator, the exciter, the rear cavity, or the hanger.

## 1. Priority

One order:

1. Forward sound of the wall-mounted picture.
2. Footprint **320 × 400 mm**.
3. Safe wall attachment of the finished mass.
4. Voice, radar, environment, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, PoE, and Home Assistant.

There is no product-mass ceiling. Panel moving mass is still an acoustic variable: extra mass belongs in the frame and the rear wall, not in the radiator and not in extra motors.

## 2. Envelope

- Width and height stay **320 × 400 mm**.
- Body thickness target remains 40 mm.
- Acoustic allowance: **+8 mm**. Hard maximum **48 mm**, only for the stack in section 5.
- The allowance is not a woofer box. A cavity a few litres deep cannot be a high-fidelity low-bass enclosure, and this revision does not add a cone woofer.

## 3. Radiator

One sandwich panel, active area **300 × 380 mm**, radiates forward only.

Baseline stack remains A1, because it matches the DML material rule (thin, light, high compressive strength, useful bending stiffness, non-magnetic):

- skins: Gurit SE75 / EGL300, [0/90] and [90/0], about 0.50 mm each;
- core: ROHACELL 51 IG-F, 5.0 mm;
- structural thickness about 6.0 mm;
- preliminary structural mass about **241 g** before adhesive, fabric, and the exciter.

Stack B and Stack C stay comparison cases. A metal skin is not the baseline. Radar no longer has a veto over a better-sounding non-conductive stack; it moves to the border instead.

Edge condition: continuous compliant perimeter (PORON 4701-30 family or a measured equivalent). No hard clamp. The visible frame must not become a second clamp. Rounded panel corners are allowed if they reduce edge reflections without shrinking the radiating area.

## 4. Exciter

Production baseline: **one Dayton Audio EX25FHE2-4**.

Manufacturer data used here:

- 4 ohm, Re 4.3 ohm, Le 0.10 mH, BL 3.63 Tm;
- free-air Fs about 115 Hz;
- 24 W RMS;
- body about 58.3 × 56 × 25.5 mm;
- mass about **113 g**.

One motor is about 32% of the preliminary 241 g panel. Four DAEX25FHE-4 were about 444 g, roughly 65% of the same panel, on a regular grid. That baseline is withdrawn.

Why this part, and why only one:

- The low free-air resonance and the high BL are the properties that can drive a large light panel. Coin exciters (DAEX13/19/25CT) are lighter and thinner, but their free-air resonances sit at about 300–650 Hz and their BL is about 1.1–1.8 Tm. They are the small-surface family.
- Dayton’s placement guidance treats one offset exciter as the uniform case. A second motor is allowed only after the radiation model shows a specific hole, and only if the panel mass remains at least twice the total exciter mass. Two EX25FHE2-4 (about 226 g) fail that rule on the A1 panel.
- The second amplifier channel is left unused. Do not series-pair, do not parallel, and do not build left/right on this panel.

Thickness fallback, not the acoustic baseline: one legacy **DAEX25FHE-4** (about 110.9 g, 50.5 mm diameter, 20.5 mm tall, Fs about 224 Hz, same BL and power). Use it only if the EX25FHE2-4 stack cannot stay inside 48 mm.

## 5. Thickness at the exciter column

From the front face:

| Layer | mm |
| --- | ---: |
| Fabric allowance | 1.5 |
| Air gap, fabric to panel | 2.5 |
| Panel A1 | 6.0 |
| EX25FHE2-4 body | 25.5 |
| Clearance behind the motor | 8.0 |
| Damped rear skin | 2.5 |
| **Total** | **46.0** |

Two millimetres remain inside the 48 mm maximum for adhesive and tolerance. The rear clearance is there so the back skin does not become a second diaphragm or a buzz surface. The old stack left about 2–3 mm.

Away from that single column the cavity behind the panel is available to electronics. Voice, radar, and the environmental chamber stay on the perimeter. They do not set the exciter coordinate and they do not bridge the panel.

## 6. Placement seed

Origin: lower-left corner of the 300 × 380 mm active panel.

Dayton’s rectangular offset is 2/5 from one edge and 3/5 from the other, on both axes:

- **S0 = (120, 152) mm**

S0 is a seed, not a drawing. The radiation model may move it by ±25 mm. Keep the motor body at least 15 mm clear of the compliant land. Do not return to a 2 × 2 grid. Candidate A and Candidate B are withdrawn.

## 7. Rear cavity and the wall

The picture is a sealed forward radiator hung on the wall.

- No audio-band path from the panel’s rear volume to the room. The 22-slot chimney and the line-of-sight labyrinths are withdrawn from the acoustic cavity. A later vent is allowed only with a measured insertion loss, not with a ray screen.
- Fill the shallow rear volume with porous absorber so it does not reflect into the panel.
- The wall is the baffle. Do not leave a 3–5 mm open chimney behind the shell: that gap colors the low end and leaks. Any cleat airspace sits outside the sealed box.
- Heat leaves by conduction into the wall bracket, outside the radio keep-outs. Continuous level is whatever the exciter and the sealed thermal path survive. It is not the amplifier’s 2 × 30 W figure.

## 8. Frame and hanger

The frame is an inert baffle and the wall attachment. Stiffness and damping matter. Mass does not, except that the hanger must carry it.

- Product weight, including a tug on the frame, is reacted by the rear structure. Not by the panel, the PORON, or the exciter adhesive.
- The skin behind the exciter is not the cleat.
- Until a prototype is weighed, design the cleat, fasteners, and stated wall anchors for an **8 kg** device and a **3×** static pull. Replace 8 kg with the measured mass before any installation instruction is published. Do not assume the wall is concrete; the mount has to name the anchor for the wall it claims to support.
- Keep the center of gravity close to the wall and inside the cleat footprint.

## 9. Fabric and signal

Over the radiating area the primary cloth is **ShowTex Speaker Mesh Print, item 3838** (about 95 g/m²). ShowTex Matrix Print, item 3849, is the cosmetic candidate only where a transfer measurement shows it does not damage the response. No metallic ink. Do not bond cloth to the panel.

One unit is mono. Stereo is two units in the room, which the existing Home Assistant group already allows. DSP may flatten a repeatable response. It is not a substitute for the motor, the seal, or the missing low bass.

## 10. Amplifier

TAS5825M stays, at 48 kHz, on +24 V. Rev.B uses one BTL channel into one 4 ohm exciter. The limiter follows the 24 W RMS exciter rating until a mounted excursion and thermal model exists. The LC network stays the TI reference (10 µH / 0.68 µF class) until mounted Z(f) is measured or computed. PoE still cannot be treated as the full external-supply output.

## 11. Next model, in this order

1. Orthotropic A1 panel, compliant edge, one EX25FHE2-4 at S0, motor mass on the real footprint.
2. Surface velocity and forward radiation at 0.5 m and 1 m, wall-baffled, rear cavity sealed and absorbed.
3. Move S0 only if that radiation improves. Do not add a second exciter unless section 4’s mass rule still holds.
4. Export mounted Z(f) and only then retune the LC filter.
5. Size the hanger from the resulting mass. Structural FEA of the old EU frames is not an input.

Design objectives for that model, not measured results:

- strong forward energy from about 80 Hz upward once the picture is on the wall;
- no raw hole deeper than 12 dB from 100 Hz to 16 kHz inside the listening window at 1 m;
- no peak that needs more than 6 dB of DSP boost to enter a ±3 dB window;
- no contact between cloth and panel at rated power;
- rear skin clear of the motor at the 8 mm minimum.

Below about 70 Hz this area and this motor do not have a high-fidelity displacement reserve. The objective does not promise that band.

## 12. Withdrawn

- Four DAEX25FHE-4 in two series 8 ohm pairs.
- EX25FHE2-4 as a drop-in set of four. One successor motor replaces the set.
- Candidate A and Candidate B, and any left/right pairing on one panel.
- 40 mm as a hard maximum.
- Frame mass as a rejection gate, including the removed 250 g ceiling and the stale 1.55 kg system budget.
- Rear chimney, 22 vents, and geometric labyrinths as acoustic design.
- Matrix Print as the default cloth over the radiator.
- Voice, radar, or vent geometry as a reason to move the exciter.
