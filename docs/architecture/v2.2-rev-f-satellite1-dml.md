# AudioPicture V2.2 Rev.F — Satellite1.1 brain, DML ≤ 30 mm

Status: **SUPERSEDED_BY_REV_G / NOT_AN_ACOUSTIC_MEASUREMENT**

Rev.G drops the external TAS5825M and the second exciter. The Satellite1 amplifier drives one Visaton EX 60 S – 4. See [v2.2-rev-g-clean.md](v2.2-rev-g-clean.md).

Rev.F keeps the Rev.E acoustic envelope and replaces the custom MAIN/XVF/PoE stack with the **Satellite1.1 Dev Kit** (FutureProof Homes) plus a **dedicated two-channel DML amplifier**.

**Hard envelope:** 320 × 400 × **≤ 30 mm**. No cone drivers. No DAEX32SQ-class heavy midrange exciter as the bass motor.

## 1. Split of responsibilities

| Block | Source | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compute, Wi-Fi, voice, mics, mute, buttons | Satellite1.1 Dev Kit (HAT + Core) | ESP32-S3, XMOS XU316, 4 mics, ESPHome, Assist, Snapcast |
| Climate and light | On the HAT | AHT20 temperature/humidity, LTR-303 illuminance |
| Presence | LD2450 on the Sat1 header | 24 GHz mmWave, firmware already supported. Not the old 60 GHz BGT60 |
| Power | USB-C PD into the HAT, **≥ 45 W**, negotiated **20 V** | Feeds the kit and the external amp. No PoE, no Ethernet |
| Panel | Rev.E honeycomb diaphragm | The radiator. See `mechanical/dml-panel-rev-e.md` |
| Motors | EX_LF + EX_WB, each ≤ 16 mm deep | Two specialised DML exciters, not one Dayton DAEX32 |
| Amplifier | TAS5825M, dual BTL, I2S in | Channel A → EX_LF, channel B → EX_WB. The kit’s mono TAS2780 is **not** connected to the panel |

The Satellite1 finished speaker (110 × 110 × 181 mm, 3″ woofer, port) is **not** used. Only the Dev Kit boards.

## 2. Why the kit amp is not the panel amp

The HAT amplifier is one TAS2780, about 25 W into one voice coil. A two-exciter DML needs two processed channels and a crossover from measurement. The mono output would sum EX_LF and EX_WB and throw away the acoustic design.

Preferred signal path:

1. Confirm on the Satellite1 schematic that I2S (BCLK, LRCK, DOUT) is on the 40-pin header or FX23L. Do this before laying the amp board.
2. Feed that I2S into the TAS5825M. PVDD is the 20 V PD rail (TAS5825M accepts it).
3. Load the Rev.E/F DML coefficient set (provisional header `ap_model_curve_rev_e.h` until the first panel is measured).

If I2S is not brought out, the fallback is the PCM5122 line output into a stereo Class-D that has its own DSP. That path is second choice. Do not wire both exciters in parallel on the TAS2780.

## 3. Acoustics (unchanged bars)

- Panel aperture ≥ 280 × 360 mm, 8–10 mm honeycomb, fabric ShowTex 3838 class.
- Prototype motors, catalog, until the OEM pair arrives:
  - EX_WB: Tectonic **TEAX25C10-8/HS** (19 mm, ~85 g, ~100 Hz–20 kHz).
  - EX_LF: a thin woofer-class exciter ≤ 16 mm (OEM or Billionsound RMD-class). Not DAEX32SQ-8 (22.5 mm, 130 g, Fs 481 Hz).
- Targets: ~50 Hz–20 kHz after DSP, ≥ 95 dB short-term above 100 Hz, wide coverage.
- Stack: fabric 1.5 + panel 8–10 + motor ≤ 16 + rear skin. Total ≤ 30 mm. The HAT (10 mm) sits in the **bottom service rail**, beside the Core, not stacked on the motors.

## 4. What Rev.F drops

These were Rev.E requirements. They do not fit the Satellite1 kit, so they are withdrawn rather than pretended:

- Ethernet W5500 and PoE.
- Custom XVF3800 voice board (the XMOS XU316 replaces it).
- 60 GHz BGT60/LD6004 (presence is the LD2450).
- INA228 rail telemetry, unless added later on the amp board.
- Custom ESP-IDF image as the first firmware. Product firmware is **ESPHome**, factory image of the kit, plus a small TAS5825M init (ESPHome external component or a tiny coprocessor). Our `room_audio` C stays the coefficient reference, not the boot firmware.
- Sub-50 µs custom clock. Multi-room sync is **Snapcast** (and Sendspin when the kit supports it). Stereo LEFT/RIGHT is two pictures in one Music Assistant / Snapcast group, with per-unit delay once the ESPHome config can write the TAS delay. The HA `room.py` planner still computes the numbers.

## 5. Mechanical integration

- Service rail along the bottom 52 mm, inside the 30 mm depth: HAT, Core, TAS5825M board, USB-C inlet on the bottom edge, LD2450 window on the frame.
- Mic ports of the HAT must see the room through the frame, not through the vibrating panel.
- Mute button remains the kit’s hardware mute.
- The 24-LED ring is optional and only if it fits the frame lip. It is not an acoustic part.
- French cleat, 5 kg working load × 3, unchanged.
- CAD kernel `mechanical/rev-e/cad/` is still the envelope. A Rev.F DMU adds the Sat1 keep-out (88 × 88 × 10 mm HAT) in the rail.

## 7. Open before a second unit

1. Satellite1 pinout: I2S present or not.
2. Measured panel + the two exciters, then freeze TAS coefficients.
3. ESPHome YAML: voice, sensors, LD2450, Snapcast, TAS init, mute.
4. Confirm 20 V PD and TAS5825M share a rail without browning out the XMOS.
5. Mic SNR with the panel playing.

## 8. Withdrawn

- Driving the picture from the Satellite1 mono speaker output.
- Re-using the Satellite1 ported enclosure or its 3″ woofer and tweeter.
- DAEX32SQ-8 as the bass exciter.
- Rev.E custom MAIN as the first build. Rev.E remains the acoustic specification.
