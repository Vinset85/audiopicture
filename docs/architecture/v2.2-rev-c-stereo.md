# AudioPicture V2.2 Rev.C — Stereo two-way

Status: **SUPERSEDED_BY_REV_D**

Mono two-way and room software authority is `docs/architecture/v2.2-rev-d-mono-room.md`. Left and right in one picture are withdrawn.

This revision replaced Rev.B. The picture was a sealed stereo loudspeaker on the wall. The DML panel and the single exciter were withdrawn.

Footprint stays **320 × 400 mm**. Body thickness becomes **84 mm maximum**. That is the smallest stack that clears the motors and the linear excursion. A 48 mm body cannot hold these drivers.

## 1. What “stereo” means here

Left and right are separate motors, separate air, separate signals. Nothing is summed to mono.

Tweeter centers sit **36 mm** in from each outer edge. Spacing is **248 mm**. At 2 m the pair subtends about **7°**. That is real two-channel stereo and it is wider than a single compact Sonos box. It is not a pair of speakers placed across the room. A second picture remains the way to open a wide stage.

Each tweeter is aimed **15° outward** behind the cloth.

## 2. Transducers

Per channel, mirrored:

| Role | Part | Qty | Data used |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Woofer | Dayton Audio ND91-4 | 1 | 4 ohm, Fs 74 Hz, Qts 0.41, Vas 1.4 L, Sd 30.4 cm², Xmax 4.6 mm, 85.6 dB @ 2.83 V/1 m, 30 W RMS, overall depth 63.4 mm, mounting depth 56.8 mm |
| Tweeter | Dayton Audio ND20FB-4 | 1 | 19 mm textile dome, 4 ohm, 90 dB @ 2.83 V/1 m, 15 W RMS, Fs about 2.1 kHz, overall depth 19 mm, response quoted from 3.5 kHz |
| Passive radiator | Dayton Audio ND90-PR | 1 | Sd 31.2 cm², Xmax 9 mm, Vas 1.5 L, moving mass 8.1 g plus the supplied disks, overall depth 60.7 mm |

One ND90-PR displaces about 28 cm³. One ND91 displaces about 12 cm³. The passive stays at or above twice the woofer’s linear volume.

The ND91 is a full-range cone and it beams and breaks up. It is crossed to the dome. It is not used alone to 17 kHz.

## 3. Thickness

Flanges are countersunk 2 mm. Motor depth is measured from the baffle face and already includes the baffle, so the baffle is not an extra layer.

| Layer | mm |
| --- | ---: |
| Cloth | 1.5 |
| Air in front of the baffle, woofer at +4.6 mm and passive at +9 mm | 12.2 |
| Deeper basket behind the baffle face (ND90-PR) | 61 |
| Clearance behind the magnet | 3 |
| Rear wall | 5 |
| **Total** | **82.7** |

One millimetre remains for adhesive and tolerance. Hard maximum **84 mm**.

The limiter holds the woofer to 4.6 mm and the passive to 9 mm. The 25 mm peak figure on the ND91 sheet is not a clearance budget.

## 4. Cabinet

Two sealed chambers. No port, no rear vent, no shared air.

- Net air per side: **1.0–1.3 L**, the range around Dayton’s published vented optimum for the ND91 (0.85 L, F3 73 Hz). The rest of the 320 × 400 × 84 mm body is brace, wall, and the center spine.
- Center spine, about 16 mm of sealed wall, splits left from right. Widen it only in a top rail for the microphones, outside both air volumes.
- Each chamber: passive toward the top, tweeter on the vertical centerline of the picture, woofer directly under the tweeter. Woofer-to-tweeter center distance **≤ 70 mm**.
- Stuff the 1.0–1.3 L with polyester or wool. Do not block the passive.
- Passive tuning mass is not frozen. First target is an acoustic F3 near **65 Hz** before DSP. Confirm in a box model, then on the bench.
- The wall is the baffle. Do not open a chimney behind the shell.

## 5. Crossover, amp, DSP

TAS5825M stays: one BTL channel per side, 48 kHz, +24 V.

Each channel feeds a passive crossover placed after the Class-D LC filter.

- Acoustic crossover **3 kHz**, second order, tweeter padded to the woofer’s level and protected from excursion below 3 kHz.
- Nominal load **4 ohm**, not below **3.2 ohm** at any audio frequency.
- The Rev.A 10 µH / 0.68 µF network was for an 8 ohm exciter pair. Retune it for this load. Do not copy it.

DSP on the TAS5825M, before the passive crossover:

- wall-mounted response, not a free-standing baffle-step curve;
- cut the ND91 breakup above the crossover;
- bass boost only inside woofer and passive excursion;
- channel trim so left and right match.

PoE does not get the external 24 V level. The excursion limiter is the same on both supplies.

## 6. Design objectives

These are the bars for the box model and the first measured sample. They are not results.

- 60 Hz–20 kHz within ±3 dB at 1 m, on the center axis, at 80 dB, after DSP.
- 90 dB short-term at 1 m from 100 Hz up, inside the 30 W woofer rating.
- Left and right both play, polarity marked, no electrical sum.
- No cloth contact at the limited excursion.
- Below 55 Hz the objective stops. A 1.2 L chamber and a 3.5 inch cone do not support a 45 Hz ±3 dB claim.

## 7. Everything else

Voice, radar, light, temperature, Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and Home Assistant stay. They occupy the spine and the top rail. They do not share air with either chamber and they do not move a driver.

The cloth over the drivers is ShowTex Speaker Mesh Print, item 3838, used as a grille. No metallic ink. No cloth bonded to a cone.

The hanger is on the rear wall, sized for **8 kg** and a **3×** static pull until a prototype is weighed. The cones carry none of the weight.

## 8. Withdrawn

- The DML sandwich, the PORON panel mount, and both Dayton exciters.
- Mono drive and the seed at (120, 152) mm.
- The 48 mm body.
- Candidate A/B and the four-exciter grid, already withdrawn in Rev.B.
