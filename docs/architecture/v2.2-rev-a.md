# AudioPicture V2.2 Rev.A — Architecture

## Product constraints
- External envelope: **320 x 400 x 40 mm maximum**.
- Printed acoustic fabric covers the entire front.
- Ethernet + Wi-Fi/BLE.
- PoE+ or external **24 V DC / 3 A recommended**.
- Plug-and-play Home Assistant installation; no user YAML or manual IP configuration.
- Local-first core operation, OTA and recovery.

## PCB partition
1. **PCB-A MAIN** — ESP32-S3, W5500, PoE/power path, INA228, DC/DC, TAS5825M.
2. **PCB-B VOICE** — XVF3800 and 4 PDM microphones in XMOS SQ66 geometry.
3. **PCB-C RADAR** — BGT60TR13C, local 1.8 V rail and level translation.
4. **PCB-D ENV** — SHT45-AD1F and OPT3004.

## Power tree
PoE+ -> Ag53024 -> protected back-to-back-MOSFET disconnect/priority path.
External 24 V -> fuse/transient/reverse protection -> protected ideal-diode path.
Both -> 24V_RAW -> Kelvin shunt + INA228 -> 24V_SYS.
24V_SYS -> TAS5825M and TPSM63603 5 V.
5 V -> 3.3 V buck.
3.3 V -> local low-noise rails and radar 1.8 V.

USB-C is service/recovery and logic power only; it does not power the DML amplifier.

## Audio
System target: 48 kHz. TAS5825M drives two nominal 8-ohm series pairs of 4-ohm DML exciters. Four Dayton DAEX25FHE-4 exciters are used.

Voice and acoustic measurement are separate signal paths. Raw microphone calibration data must bypass AEC, AGC, noise suppression and beamforming.

## Voice
XMOS XVF3800 with four PDM MEMS microphones. Baseline geometry is the XMOS-characterized SQ66: a 66 x 66 mm square array. Microphone power has a physical hardware cut-off for privacy.

## Presence and environment
- BGT60TR13C 60 GHz radar for presence and micro-motion.
- SHT45-AD1F for temperature/RH in a thermally isolated passive air chamber.
- OPT3004 for ambient light behind calibrated fabric.

## Home Assistant
One physical AudioPicture appears as one HA device. Target entities include media player, Assist/voice, temperature, humidity, illuminance, presence, network, power source/consumption, firmware/diagnostics and acoustic calibration.

Multiple units assigned to the same room can form a distributed acoustic calibration group.

## Engineering release rule
Do not release Gerbers until all VALIDATE items have been checked against current manufacturer reference designs, ERC/DRC has passed, power and thermal budgets have been reviewed, and DML/radar/microphone mechanical placement has been validated.
