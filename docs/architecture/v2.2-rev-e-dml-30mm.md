# AudioPicture V2.2 Rev.E — Ultra-thin DML, acoustic first, full room node

Status: **ARCHITECTURE_AUTHORITY / NOT_AN_ACOUSTIC_MEASUREMENT**

This revision replaces Rev.D for the acoustic radiator, body thickness, and transducer BOM. **Room software, Home Assistant roles, and the full sensor/network node from Rev.D remain in force** and are restated here so one document is complete.

**Hard envelope:** footprint **320 × 400 mm**, total thickness **≤ 30 mm** from wall contact plane to front fabric face. No cone woofer, no passive radiator, no 84 mm cabinet.

## 0. Why Rev.E

Rev.D (ND91-4 + ND20FB-4 + 2× ND90-PR in 84 mm) cannot meet ≤ 30 mm. Commercial reference for excellence near this thickness is advanced distributed-mode loudspeaker technology (e.g. Amina Sapphire375: 34 mm, claimed 50 Hz–20 kHz −6 dB). Sonos/IKEA SYMFONISK picture stayed at **60 mm** with shallow cones. Rev.E therefore adopts a **dual-exciter DML panel** as the only path that can chase both thickness and high-level bass.

Withdrawn by this revision: Rev.D cone/PR stack, P1 84 mm tub/baffle architecture as product authority, and any claim that catalog Dayton exciters alone are the acoustic answer (that was the Rev.A failure mode: exciter mass dominating the panel).

## 1. Acoustic architecture — one picture

One picture is one **excellent mono** radiator. Stereo and coverage come from two or more pictures in the same Home Assistant room (unchanged from Rev.D §4).

| Role | Requirement | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radiating diaphragm | Lightweight bending-wave panel, phenolic honeycomb or equivalent composite | Area ≈ product face minus frame lip; target radiating aperture ≥ 280 × 360 mm |
| Low-frequency motor | **1× LowFreq-class DML exciter** | Large voice coil, spiderless/engineered coupler, designed for bass on a panel — not a generic DAEX |
| Mid/high motor | **1× wideband DML / BMR-class exciter** | Complements LowFreq; placement from modal FEA, not geometric centre alone |
| Enclosure | Shallow sealed rear cavity + wall as infinite baffle | No port; cavity depth is whatever remains inside 30 mm after panel + motors |
| Front finish | Acoustically open fabric / print (ShowTex 3838 class) | No plaster skim (unlike in-wall invisible speakers); fabric loss must be measured |

### 1.1 Thickness budget (hard)

| Layer | Max allocation |
| --- | ---: |
| Front fabric + print + stretch | 1.5 mm |
| Panel diaphragm | 8–12 mm |
| Exciter + coupler protrusion behind panel | ≤ 14 mm |
| Rear skin / electronics keep-out / wall stand-off | remainder to **30.0 mm total** |

Any part that cannot meet this budget is redesigned or moved to a wall-recessed cleat cavity that does **not** increase the visible product depth in front of the wall plane. Prefer fitting everything in 30 mm; document any cleat recess as an explicit mechanical exception.

### 1.2 Acoustic design objectives (product bars)

Bars for FEA, prototypes, and the first measured sample. Not yet results.

- **50 Hz–20 kHz** within ±3 dB at 1 m, on axis, at 80 dB, after factory DSP (target aligned with best-in-class thin DML claims).
- Short-term **≥ 95 dB** at 1 m above 100 Hz inside thermal and excursion limits of the exciters.
- Below 45 Hz the objective stops; DSP high-pass (≥ 30 Hz, steep) protects the panel.
- Wide, even coverage (DML advantage): listening off-axis must not collapse highs the way a dome does.
- Distortion and power compression measured; modal ringing controlled by panel damping and DSP, not by “EQ until it looks flat on one mic”.
- Cloth contact, buzz, and frame rattles: zero at limiter threshold.

### 1.3 DSP on the unit (before room group)

TAS5825M (or successor with equal DSP capability), 48 kHz, dual BTL:

- channel A → LowFreq exciter;
- channel B → wideband exciter;
- active crossover / band split from measured panel response (not a fixed 3 kHz cone LR4);
- modal peaking EQ, factory curve, dynamic limiter on panel excursion proxies (current + model);
- wall-baffle curve, not free-standing baffle-step.

`firmware/components/room_audio` stays the host-tested logic core; the Rev.D woofer/tweeter LR4 defaults are replaced by a Rev.E DML coefficient set once measured. Roles SOLO / LEFT / RIGHT / FILL, delay, trim, and clock rules are unchanged.

### 1.4 Panel engineering rules

1. Exciter moving mass ≪ panel modal mass at the driven modes (avoid Rev.A mass dominance).
2. Dual drive points from FEA / laser vibrometry; never two exciters at the geometric centre.
3. Frame must not short-circuit bending modes (compliant mounts or tuned decoupling).
4. Electronics pocket is sealed from the rear cavity only where air leaks would add hiss or thermal pumping into sensors; microphones never share an open path with the loud panel without acoustic baffling.
5. First prototypes: three panel material candidates, three exciter placements, one fabric. Down-select on measured F3, smoothness, and max SPL before tooling.

All of the following remain product requirements. Thickness does not remove them; it forces packaging discipline.

## 3. Several pictures in one room

Unchanged policy from Rev.D:

- lone unit: SOLO, mono downmix;
- two identical units, same firmware major: LEFT / RIGHT stereo pair;
- three or more, or non-pair: shared clock, per-unit delay and level, optional FILL with precedence delay;
- clock targets: **< 50 µs** pair, **< 200 µs** group;
- calibration uses the four product microphones as a raw measurement path (bypass AEC/AGC/NS/beamforming).

Reference code already in tree: `ap_room`, `ap_clock`, `ap_dsp` (host-tested), `home-assistant/.../room.py`. Wire to I2S/TAS and HA entities under Rev.E.

## 4. Electronics packaging principles

1. **Area before depth:** spread MAIN along a bottom or side **service rail** inside the 30 mm envelope rather than stacking tall modules behind the panel centre.
2. **Thermal:** Class-D and PoE heat away from SHT45 and from the panel bond line.
3. **RF:** ESP32 antenna and radar windows clear of continuous copper and of the metallic frame where possible.
4. **Acoustic isolation:** board mounts must not rattle; no screw into a high-velocity panel region.
5. **Privacy:** mute is hardware power-kill to mics/voice rail, as in P1 flex-header policy.
