- record volume;

Legal restoration features are added only after the responsible keep-out is identified and must themselves be re-subtracted by all hard keep-outs.

## 7. Mass-load interfaces
Product mass shall not be applied to one arbitrary frame point.

Create subsystem load-transfer groups:
- LOAD_DML_PERIMETER
- LOAD_MAIN_C
- LOAD_MAIN_P
- LOAD_VOICE
- LOAD_RADAR
- LOAD_ENV
- LOAD_REAR_SHELL
- LOAD_FRONT_FRAME
- LOAD_HARNESS_MISC.

Where the real product interface is compliant, represent stiffness/contact rather than rigidly tying the entire surface.

## 8. DML structural isolation
DML PORON support:
- not a wall-mount structural shortcut;
- may transfer its actual product mass/reaction into frame;
- shall not be modeled as a rigid diaphragm that artificially stiffens the frame.

For frame-only first solve:
use distributed equivalent mass/reaction at actual support land.

For coupled solve:
use calibrated compliant perimeter elements/contact.

BCSET_LC2_R:

## 10. Contact definitions

- realistic mount contact;
- no giant fully-fixed rear face.

- first pass bonded/elastic insert interface;
- sensitivity to reduced stiffness;
- coupon-calibrated model before release.

- wall-normal support;

- ASA shell cannot become an accidental primary structural member.

## 12. CAD validity tests
Before export:
- exactly one primary structural body;
- closed-shell validity true;
- manifold edge count valid;
- zero free internal sheets;
- zero self-intersections;
- zero negative/invalid volumes;
- minimum feature thickness >= process rule except intentionally compliant necks;
- all named interfaces non-empty;
- all hard keep-outs have positive clearance.

## 13. Topology persistence tests
Regenerate at:
- P_CLEAT 26 mm;
- P_CLEAT 30 mm;
- P_CLEAT 36 mm;
- boss OD 11/12/13 mm;
- rail web 2.4/2.8/3.2 mm.

For each:
- rebuild without manual repair;
- preserve semantic groups;
- preserve one primary body;
- no keep-out violation.

A parameter is not production-parametric if it requires manual face reselection.

## 16. Solver result schema
For every case record:
- geometry revision;
- material case;
- temperature case;
- print orientation;
- mesh size/count;
- max displacement;
- named-point displacements;
- non-singular stress/strain maxima;
- orthotropic failure index;
- reaction per M4/cleat;
- lower-pad reaction;
- anti-lift reaction;
- buckling factor where applicable;
- mass;
- convergence delta;
- hotspot classification.

## 17. Release thresholds
LC1:
- calibrated margin >=2.0
- upper mount displacement <=0.5 mm.

LC2..LC6:
- calibrated margin >=1.5 unless superseded by stricter subsystem requirement.

LC4:
- corner wall-normal displacement <=1.0 mm.

LC7:
- no assembly preload state creates local failure or mount disengagement.

All:
- no disconnected load path;
- no insert pull-through;
- no unstable contact opening;
- no hard keep-out violation.
