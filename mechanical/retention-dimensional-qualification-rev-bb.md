## Pilot sample
Minimum five coupons per material/process condition is recommended only for initial screening.

Five samples are not, by themselves, a production capability study.

Do not claim Cp/Cpk from the pilot set.

## Full-part requirement
The small coupons cannot reproduce:
- 320 x 400 ASA shell warpage;
- complete PC-CF frame distortion;
- location-dependent effects across the six retention nodes.

Therefore full-part metrology is mandatory.

For each assembled shell/frame pair measure the six nodes:
TOP_L, TOP_R, SIDE_L, SIDE_R, BOT_L, BOT_R.

Record the local uncompressed gap before fastener preload.

## Spacer-class release logic
Do not choose spacer classes from nominal CAD.

After measured data exist:
1. separate measurement error from part variation where practical;
2. calculate observed min/max and distribution per node;
3. determine whether one common spacer family can cover all six nodes;
4. if needed, define graded spacer thicknesses;
5. reserve compliant trim only for residual variation;
6. verify that hard-stop height prevents ASA over-compression.

## Production capability
A release capability study requires:
- frozen printer/material/profile/orientation;
- conditioned parts;
- controlled measurement method;
- statistically justified sample size;
- acceptance limits derived from functional clamp requirements.

Those limits do not yet exist.
