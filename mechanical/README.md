# Mechanical

Fixed product envelope: **320 x 400 x 40 mm maximum**.

Baseline:
- printed acoustic-fabric removable front frame;
- DML panel approximately 300 x 380 mm;
- four DAEX25FHE-4 exciters;
- microphone PCB mechanically isolated from DML vibration;
- radar behind fabric with RF keep-out;
- environmental PCB in a thermally isolated passive air chamber;
- rear connection bay for RJ45, 24 V DC and USB-C service.

Exciter, radar and microphone mechanical coordinates are not production-frozen until simulation/validation.
