# AudioPicture V2.2 — printed acoustic fabric selection Rev.A

Status: **PRINTABLE_ACOUSTIC_FABRIC_PRIMARY_SELECTED / FINAL_TRANSFER_FUNCTION_MEASUREMENT_REQUIRED**

## 1. Primary candidate
Primary:
**ShowTex Matrix Print, item 3849**

Published characteristics:
- intended as sound-transparent acoustic print fabric / speaker cloth;
- direct dye-sublimation printing;
- 67% recycled PET + 33% PES;
- 205 g/m2;
- width 320 cm;
- printable width 315 cm;
- EN 13501-1 B-s1,d0;
- OEKO-TEX 100 Class 4.

Reason for selection:
it is explicitly designed for printed acoustic/speaker applications and offers a finer decorative textile concept than large perforated speaker mesh.

## 2. Secondary qualification candidate
**ShowTex Speaker Mesh Print, item 3838**

Published:
- perforated printable speaker cloth;
- 100% PES;
- 95 g/m2 at 310 cm width;
- mesh approximately 4 x 2 mm;
- dye-sub or UV roll-to-roll printing;
- EN 13501-1 B-s1,d0;
- OEKO-TEX Class 2.

Use as acoustic-transparency fallback if Matrix Print attenuation or microphone transfer is unacceptable.

## 3. Print baseline
For Matrix Print:
direct dye-sub process baseline.

No metallic/conductive ink or foil.

Artwork coverage may be full-area but qualification includes worst dark/high-ink region.

## 4. Mass impact
Visible product area:
0.32 x 0.40 = 0.128 m2.

At 205 g/m2:
front-area textile mass ~26.2 g before wrap allowance.

With rear wrap/trim, working installed fabric mass budget:
**30..38 g**.

This remains compatible with the <=120 g complete front-frame subsystem target.

## 5. Optical issue
Matrix Print is not assumed optically transparent enough for OPT3004.

The exact artwork over the optical path must be calibrated.

Preferred:
controlled locally lighter print/open optical micro-region while retaining visual concealment.

## 6. Microphone issue
Marketing/acoustic transparency does not prove flat microphone transfer.

Measure printed fabric transfer for the IM72D128 acoustic path.

DSP may compensate broad repeatable response only after SNR/directivity impact is acceptable.

## 7. Radar issue
Polyester textile is preferred to metallic decoration, but 60 GHz compatibility is not assumed.

Test final printed fabric and carrier in radar R0..R4 sequence.

## 8. DML qualification
Measure A0..A4:
A0 no fabric
A1 unprinted Matrix Print
A2 representative printed artwork
A3 worst ink-density area
A4 complete tensioned carrier.

Record SPL/phase 100 Hz..20 kHz and buzz/rattle.

## 9. Tension process
Do not freeze a numeric N/m tension without supplier/process coupon data.

Qualification criterion:
- no visible sag;
- no contact with DML;
- carrier warp remains within gap tolerance;
- artwork distortion acceptable;
- acoustic response repeatable.
